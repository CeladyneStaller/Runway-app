Signed is not spent.

A purchase order payable in nine months does not change your runway — the money has not moved. It should still change your decision. Waterline shows both, and does not confuse them.

Two numbers, not one

Runway is when your cash runs out. It keeps meaning exactly that, because every alert, chart and comparison in the product depends on it.

Covered runway is how long the cash lasts if you also honour everything you have signed. The gap between the two is the period in which the model says you are fine and your signatures say otherwise.

Before you sign
