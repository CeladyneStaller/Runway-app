What sits below the line

Four things decide how deep you are riding.

Each one lands on the month the money moves, not the month the paperwork was signed.

Grants

An award is not a deposit

Period of performance, drawdown schedule, and the lag between the invoice you send and the money that lands. Cost share accrues against what you bill, per budget period.
