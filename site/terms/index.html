The arrangement, in the fewest words that are still true.

This is a plain-language draft, not reviewed by a lawyer. It describes what the software actually does, which is the part I can be accurate about. It should be reviewed before the first paying customer, and this notice removed when it has been.

What you get Access to Waterline for the companies you have subscribed, for as long as the subscription is current.
What it costs The plan price shown at checkout, billed monthly, cancellable from the billing screen at any time. Cancelling stops the next charge; it does not refund the current month.
Your data is yours You own your model. You can export it at any time. If a subscription lapses the model becomes read-only and exportable — it is not deleted and not withheld.
Uptime No contractual guarantee is offered. Waterline is run by one person and outages will be communicated honestly rather than quietly.
It is a model, not advice Waterline projects cash from figures you supply. It is not financial, legal or accounting advice, and no decision should rest on it alone.
Ending it You may stop at any time. Accounts may be suspended for non-payment or for abuse, with notice and with an opportunity to export first.
